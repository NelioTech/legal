# Nelio Activity - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-11**

Nelio Tech LLC (contact@nelio-tech.com) operates Nelio Activity. This notice explains what the app and its service providers process. We do not sell your personal data. The free, Standard and Premium features use different data flows.

## 1. Data kept on your device

The app stores settings, preferred and excluded categories, selected moods, recommendation and interaction history, usage counters, and local diagnostic entries in its private storage. Paid-plan personalization uses local interaction history. These items are not automatically uploaded as a history or profile. However, the current settings and selected mood are sent with an analysis request as described below. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to your device's backup settings.

## 2. Analysis and location

Every analysis request reaches our server with the selected mood and intention, activity size, optional duration, city and country, current recommendation settings (including budgets, distance limits, preferred/excluded categories and language), and location coordinates if you choose to use device location. Paid requests also include an anonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier so the server can verify the subscription. The server receives the phone's time zone on paid requests. Your IP address is visible to the server and its hosting provider as part of the network connection.

Free recommendations are generated by server-side rules. For paid recommendations, our server sends a prompt containing the selected mood, preferences, city, and any supplied precise coordinates to a configured AI provider. The prompt may also contain current weather and local time. We do not send your full session history to the AI provider. Location permission can be denied or withdrawn in device settings; you can enter a city instead. If location confirmation is enabled in the app, you can decline a proposed location before analysis.

## 3. Weather and real events

On paid plans, when coordinates are supplied, our server rounds them to one decimal place (approximately 10 km) before requesting weather from MET Norway. For US locations it may also use the US National Weather Service. These providers see our server's IP address and the rounded coordinates, not the phone's IP address.

Premium event discovery sends the city, country, language and a 24-hour time window to the Ticketmaster Discovery API. Precise coordinates are used only on our server to filter returned events by distance; they are not sent to Ticketmaster.

When configured, Standard and Premium event discovery sends Perplexity the city and country, a 24-hour window, broad activity categories, maximum budget and currency, optional duration, and general weather conditions if available. We do not send Perplexity the raw mood, precise GPS coordinates, RevenueCat identifier or session history. Perplexity sees our server's IP address. Event results may be unavailable or inaccurate; verify time, price and availability with the organizer.

## 4. Advertising, purchases and diagnostics

The free plan uses Appodeal and its mediated ad partners. Depending on permissions and consent choices, they may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address, approximate or precise location, and app/ad usage data. The app provides an advertising privacy control; see [Appodeal's privacy policy](https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy) and [SDK data disclosures](https://docs.appodeal.com/android/data-protection/app-privacy-details). Paid subscribers do not see in-app ads.

Purchases are handled by the app store and RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat's anonymous app-user identifier to check entitlement; we do not receive payment-card details. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

The app stores errors and slowdowns locally and may send a weekly diagnostic summary to our server. That summary includes timestamps, feature context and sample error messages, so it should not be treated as guaranteed anonymous. The server may log operational errors and diagnostic summaries; its hosting provider may retain connection logs. We do not maintain a permanent database of analysis requests or device identifiers for recommendations.

## 5. Purposes, legal bases and retention

We process analysis inputs, location you provide and subscription status to deliver the requested service; optional precise-location access and personalized advertising depend on the relevant permission or consent choice. We process limited diagnostics to maintain service reliability. Local data remains until cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backups. Recommendation and event results may be held briefly in server memory caches (normally seconds to minutes). Diagnostic and infrastructure logs are subject to operational retention settings; contact us for the current period. Providers retain data under their own terms. In particular, [Perplexity says](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/agent-api/conversation-state) that `store: false` hides retrieval but does not prevent its server-side persistence. We cannot promise zero retention by providers.

Service providers may process data outside your country. Their policies and applicable transfer safeguards govern those transfers. We do not require a Nelio account, collect biometric data or voice recordings, or receive payment-card details.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can deny or withdraw location permission, manage advertising consent in the app, and remove local data by uninstalling. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection regarding personal data we control. Contact contact@nelio-tech.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. For data held independently by an ad network, app store or other provider, its privacy controls may also be needed.

We will update this notice when our processing changes and show the revision date above. A privacy notice does not replace any consent required by law.
