# Nelio — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-12**

We follow a simple rule: minimum data, kept locally, never used for advertising.

---

## 1. What stays on your device

The following data is stored exclusively in the app's private local storage and never leaves your phone:

- Your activity preferences and excluded categories
- Session history and past recommendations
- Subscription status and usage counters
- Behavioural interaction logs (activities viewed, decisions made, view duration) — used solely to personalise future recommendations on paid plans
- Local issue logs (errors, slowdowns) — aggregated anonymously before any transmission

## 2. What is briefly sent to third-party services

When you launch an analysis, the following is transmitted:

- Your selected mood, intention, activity size, and optional maximum duration
- Your entered city name
- Your GPS coordinates (only if you explicitly confirm the location prompt)

This data is sent to **our server** and then to **our AI service provider** to generate recommendations.

On **paid plans**, your city or GPS coordinates are also sent to our **weather data provider** to retrieve real-time weather context. This data is not retained by the provider beyond the immediate request.

**Anonymous issue reports** (aggregated errors and slowdowns — no personal data, no identifiers) are transmitted to our server approximately once a week if local issues have been recorded.

## 3. Third-party services

We use exactly two categories of external services:

| Service | Purpose | Plans |
|---|---|---|
| AI service provider | Generating activity recommendations | All plans |
| Weather data provider | Real-time weather context | Paid plans only |

No analytics platform, no advertising tracker, and no crash reporter are embedded in the app.

We intentionally do not name specific providers in this policy so that we can switch providers freely without requiring policy updates. To learn which providers are currently in use, contact us at contact@nelio-tech.com.

## 4. Data retention

Our server does not log your analysis requests.

Our AI service provider may temporarily retain prompt data for up to 30 days per their standard API terms. This data is not used for model training.

## 5. What we do NOT collect

- Email address or account information
- Advertising or device identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
- Biometric data, voice recordings, or photos
- Browsing history or any data outside the app
- Payment card information (handled entirely by the app store)

## 6. Your rights

- You can disable location access at any time in your device's system settings.
- You can decline the GPS confirmation prompt before each analysis.
- You can clear all local data by uninstalling the app.
- For any privacy questions or data requests, contact us at contact@nelio-tech.com.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
